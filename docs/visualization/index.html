What you are watching

This is a recording of the real programs, not a mock-up. The exchange, the market maker and three simulated groups of traders ran as separate processes talking over TCP and UDP. The exchange logged every order, fill and trade with a timestamp, and this page plays that log back.

Each moving dot is one message. Orders travel left to right into the matching engine; fills travel back to whoever traded. Every order is written to the journal before it is matched. Trades and book changes leave on the market data feed.

  • Buy orders and bids
  • Sell orders and asks
  • The market maker's quotes and fills
